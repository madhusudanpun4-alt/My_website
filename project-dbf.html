Competition · 2024 – 2025

AIAA Design Build Fly Competition

Aerodynamics Design Team Technical Documentation

Overview

The AIAA Design Build Fly (DBF) competition challenges university teams to design, build, and fly a radio-controlled aircraft that meets a specific mission profile under strict rules on weight, dimensions, and performance. As a member of the design team, the work centered on the aerodynamic analysis behind the aircraft's configuration.

Approach

  • Collaborated with a multidisciplinary team — spanning aerodynamics, structures, and propulsion — to deliver a cohesive aircraft design under competition constraints.
  • Contributed aerodynamic analysis to inform key design decisions across the aircraft's configuration.
  • Produced technical documentation to support design reviews and competition submission requirements.
  • Took part in project planning to keep the team's design work aligned with competition milestones and deadlines.

Role

Design Team Member, working alongside teammates across disciplines to move the aircraft from concept through a competition-ready design, with a focus on aerodynamic analysis and documentation.
