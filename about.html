About

Hi, I'm Madhusudan Pun.

I'm an Aerospace Engineer and a graduate of IOE, Pulchowk Campus, focused on autonomous navigation and experimental aerodynamics. I like problems where a state-estimation algorithm has to prove itself in simulation before it ever flies, and where a flow-control theory has to survive contact with a wind tunnel.

I've led development of a Visual-Inertial Odometry platform for GPS-denied UAV navigation, fusing IMU and stereo-camera data for 6-DOF pose estimation, validated in ROS and Gazebo. Before that, I spent time at the Indian Institute of Science processing Particle Image Velocimetry data to characterize vortex generator performance.
